# Illustration Example Quality Notes

Use these notes to calibrate finish and composition. The referenced images are visual evidence, not templates and not authoritative sources for city facts.

## Strong external references

### Spain

Asset: [Spain cover](../../../assets/references/omarchy-spain.png)

Strengths:

- dense yet legible architectural panorama;
- multiple landmark silhouettes connected by one continuous ground and reflection line;
- warm regional palette on a black field;
- small central character gives the city scale;
- fine linework remains subordinate to the wordmark.

Risk: too many landmarks can become a tourism collage if the composition lacks a unifying landscape or narrative.

### Melbourne

Asset: [Melbourne cover](../../../assets/references/omarchy-melbourne.png)

Strengths:

- foreground tram, midground station and river, background skyline;
- coherent local transport and architectural story;
- windows, rails, clouds, reflections, and masonry create full-size reward;
- restrained information rail gives the poster a complete system finish.

Density anatomy:

- **foreground:** tram body, rails, curb, and overhead-line direction;
- **midground:** station facade, bridge, river edge, palms, and observation wheel;
- **background:** varied tower silhouettes, lit windows, clouds, and night gradient;
- **connective texture:** rail perspective, masonry modules, water reflections, and repeated window grids bind the layers into one scene.

The lesson is not to copy Melbourne's number of landmarks. It is to give one local narrative enough structural, material, and atmospheric evidence that the poster feels inhabited and finished.

Risk: supporting copy and icons should be omitted for no-logistics Luma covers unless the brief explicitly allows them.

### Leiden

Asset: [Leiden cover](../../../assets/references/omarchy-leiden.png)

Strengths:

- extremely strong brand dominance;
- fine single-color skyline and mirrored-water treatment;
- clear terminal hierarchy and disciplined grid;
- sparse palette with rich micro-detail.

Risk: slogans and date/location rails belong to its original brief, not the default no-time cover contract.

## Chengdu approved direction

Asset: [Chengdu pixel city](../../../assets/meetup/chengdu-pixel-tianfu-snow-city.png)

Keep:

- non-realistic black-field pixel linework and limited flat color set;
- panda as a graphic meetup participant rather than a furry rendered mascot;
- recognizable Tianfu Twin Towers complex, snow ridge, park and lake as one layered city system;
- repeated windows, roof ribs, tree clusters and stepped reflections as structured detail;
- panda black/white, snow cool white, basin blue-gray, park green and a very small hibiscus-pink discovery.

Avoid restoring realistic sky, photographic foliage, smooth glass shading, volumetric light, or a gold-dominant sunset. A Jinsha-gold treatment belongs only to a separate concept whose subject is explicitly the Jinsha/solar-bird story.
